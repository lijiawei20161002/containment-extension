Does the probability field carry information?

Pairwise ranking error follows Cooper et al.: inside each environment, pairs of episodes with different incident counts, scored by whether the judge's mean probability orders them like the journal. Brier is compared with a constant predictor at the item prevalence; a judge no better than that constant has collapsed to its prior.

Reliability bins

Observed incident rate among items whose mean probability fell in each bin. A calibrated judge sits near the bin's midpoint; the transcript judge's confident false alarms show up as a low observed rate in the top bin.
