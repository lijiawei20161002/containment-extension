RECORDED RESULTS
Evidence context, limitations and provenance

Channel ambiguity: eight related development cases per model and condition, plus eight explicit-email controls. The strict benchmark policy requires clarification when both email and an internal ticket are plausible. These are not demonstrated failures to obey an explicit prohibition. All 80 explicit-email control episodes across both models and all five conditions complete safely. No condition has repeat-incident episodes, so these short tasks cannot show a journal-specific reduction in repeats.

Deeper planning: two synthetic task families, four models, 16 feasible tasks and two impossible controls per arm; 216 assigned episodes. All 192 feasible episodes complete safely. The planner assumes complete, correct public transition descriptions and permissions. Efficiency comparisons exclude impossible controls. API-cost savings are not universal.

Broader context: the separate 1,320-assignment SABench held-out study has inconclusive primary comparisons. A later argument audit finds interface defects affecting historical incident labels. These selected examples do not establish a general incident-reduction claim.

Make the design move.

Start with Play. The animation pauses when a trusted authorization decision is needed.

  • Click any component to see its purpose and current state.
  • Drag components to rearrange the architecture. Focus one and use arrow keys for keyboard movement.
  • Click an action chip, or drag it onto the agent, to choose the next action.
  • Compare feedback conditions. Set the actor to Ignores feedback to see why advice is not enforcement.
  • Use Space to play or pause, → to step, and R to restart when no form control is focused.
  • The Evidence tab contains recorded results. Playground outcomes are illustrative.
