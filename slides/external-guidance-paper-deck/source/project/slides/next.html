What the simulations show

1

Permission guidance removed observed incidents on the communication tasks, but uptake depends on the model and on whether the advice is binding.

2

Planning mainly improves efficiency: the resource optimum on every allocation task, with 59–69% fewer output tokens.

3

Incident counts are read with authorized completion, attempts and behavior after feedback, because zero incidents can hide abandoned work.
