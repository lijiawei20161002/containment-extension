Limitations of the evidence

Exploratory, synthetic settings

Historical experiments are concentrated in two local transition structures. All resources and effects are synthetic.

A short public benchmark

SABench tasks are short, with one sample per model, arm and task. Public data may have appeared in training.

Selected channel cases

Eight related topics and two request templates, reused from known failures. The strict clarification rule may differ from ordinary judgment.

No placebo for feedback

Arms differ in content, length and route specificity. There is no length-matched control.

Records trusted by construction

Grants and effect records are assumed correct. No compromised services, adversarial journals or wrong estimates under a binding note.

Incomplete runs

Provider errors and refusals leave unknown outcomes. They stay in the denominator and are never counted as clean.
